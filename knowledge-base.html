How measurement works

Scientific instruments convert physical quantities into signals, then convert signals into numbers. Browser instruments use device sensors that are sampled in software, which keeps the pipeline local and transparent.

Accuracy and offline use

Accuracy varies by hardware. These tools are educational and should not replace professional calibration equipment.
