Scientific principle

Reading the live charts

Barometric altitude follows the international pressure model: pressure falls with height at a known rate, so the device converts pressure into an estimated height using an assumed sea-level pressure, by default 1013.25 hPa, with temperature corrections applied.

Practical applications

Hikers use it for route planning, meteorologists for altitude-aware weather tracking, aviation for situational awareness and surveyors for preliminary height estimates.

Accuracy and disclaimer

This tool is for educational purposes and should not replace professional calibration equipment. Manual sea-level pressure and environmental corrections affect accuracy.
