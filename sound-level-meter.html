Scientific principle

Reading the live charts

A microphone converts sound pressure into an electrical signal, and the browser samples that signal to derive loudness in decibels relative to 20 micropascals, the human hearing threshold. Root-mean-square, peak and A or C weighted values describe amplitude, while an FFT separates the signal into bands from 20 Hz to 20 kHz.

Practical applications

The meter suits workplace noise assessment, environmental acoustics, hearing protection planning, classroom sound and field audio monitoring. Repeated or long-duration exposure is where measurement becomes meaningful.

Accuracy and disclaimer

This tool is for educational purposes and should not replace professional calibration equipment. Microphone placement and room treatment materially change results.
