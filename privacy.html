Overview

Field Instruments is an educational instrument project. We build browser-based tools that measure the physical world using the sensors your device already has. This policy explains what happens to information when you open those tools, what the site does not collect, and how advertising cookies are handled.

The short version is simple: sensor data is processed locally in your browser and is not sent to us for storage or analysis. The longer version below covers permissions, hosting logs, advertising and your rights.

Local processing and sensor permissions

All sensor data, including microphone input, barometric pressure, magnetometer vectors, acceleration samples and location coordinates, is processed locally on your device. Raw readings and derived calculations are kept in memory for the duration of your session. They are never written to a server we control, and nothing you measure is transmitted to Field Instruments for storage or analytics.

Your browser may ask for permission before a sensor becomes available. Microphone access is required for the sound level meter, motion and orientation access for the vibration and star tools, and location access for the star finder and GPS comparison. The permission prompt is controlled by your browser and operating system. You can deny access, revoke it at any time in your browser settings, and still use any tool that does not depend on the denied sensor.

The CSV export is generated inside the browser from data already in memory. The file is saved by your browser and remains under your control. Closing the tab ends the session and discards the in-memory measurements. The design goal is explicit: the tools should work like a scientific instrument that lives inside your browser, with the privacy boundary drawn at your device.

What we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, account credentials, payment information, health data, address books, or the contents of your microphone beyond the in-memory acoustic analysis needed to draw the meter. No account is required to use any instrument. We do not build user profiles from sensor readings, and we do not sell personal information.

We do not knowingly collect information from children. Geolocation is used locally to place the sky or compare altitudes; it is not stored by us. If you send an email, the message and the address you use are handled by the email provider so that a reply can be sent.

Cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small file that a website or an advertising partner stores in your browser. Field Instruments does not set cookies to identify you or to remember sensor measurements. Some cookies may be set by Google advertising services, as described below. Your browser also provides local storage and similar technologies; the site does not use them to build a personal profile.

Google AdSense and DoubleClick DART cookies

Field Instruments displays content through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, including the DoubleClick Data and Remarketing (DART) cookie, to serve, measure and limit advertisements. The DART cookie allows Google to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet. These cookies are governed by Google's own privacy policy, not by this one.

We do not share personally identifying information with advertisers. We do not pass sensor readings, microphone content or precise location to advertising systems. Advertising is separated from the measurement tools: an ad cannot change a reading, a chart or a calculation.

You can control personalized advertising through your Google account settings, and you can block or delete cookies in your browser. Where consent is required by law, a consent message is presented before personalized advertising cookies are used. Declining personalized advertising does not prevent you from using the instruments.

Third-party vendors and your choices

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this site or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

For more information about how Google uses data, see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses cookies in advertising. These links lead to third-party sites whose policies we do not control.

Hosting and server logs

The site is served as static files by a hosting provider. Like most hosting providers, it may process standard request information such as an IP address, browser user agent, requested URL, timestamp and referring page for security, abuse prevention and reliable delivery. Those logs are generated by the hosting layer, not by the instrument code, and we do not combine them with sensor data. We do not use them to identify individual visitors.

Data retention

Because sensor data stays in memory, there is no measurement database to retain. Session data disappears when the tab closes or the page is reloaded. Email correspondence is kept only as long as needed to answer the message and maintain a record of technical corrections. Advertising cookies are retained according to Google's policies and your own browser settings.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of personal data, to request portability, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold a store of personal sensor data, many requests will result in a confirmation that no such record exists. Requests relating to advertising cookies should be directed to Google or handled through your browser and consent settings.

Under laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA/CPRA, you may also have the right not to be discriminated against for exercising your rights. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, contact the privacy address on the Contact page. We may ask for enough information to verify that a request is genuine, but we will not ask for sensor readings or unnecessary identity documents.

Children's privacy

The instruments are intended for a general educational audience and are not directed at children under thirteen. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information by email, contact us and the message will be removed from the correspondence record.

Security

Because no personal measurement data leaves the device, the retention risk is minimal. The site is served over HTTPS, and sensor access generally requires a secure origin. No system is perfectly secure, and email is not an encrypted channel, so please do not send sensitive personal documents. If you discover a security issue, report it through the contact page with enough detail to reproduce it.

Educational use: This tool is for educational purposes and should not replace professional calibration equipment.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site adds a feature, changes an advertising configuration or responds to a legal requirement. The date at the top of the page shows the most recent revision. Material changes will be reflected in the policy text rather than announced through a separate account notification, because the site does not maintain user accounts.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the privacy address on our Contact page or directly to privacy@fiveinstrumentsky.xyz.
