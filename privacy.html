Privacy Policy

How Field Instruments handles data when you open a sensor tool in your browser. The short version: we do not collect your data.

On this page

  • Overview
  • Local processing
  • AdSense and DART cookies
  • What we do not collect

Overview

Field Instruments is an educational instrument project. We build browser-based tools that measure the physical world using the sensors your device already has. This policy explains what happens to information when you use those tools.

Local processing

All sensor data, including microphone input, barometric pressure, magnetometer vectors and motion samples, is processed locally on your device. Raw readings and derived calculations are kept in memory for the duration of your session and are never written to a server we control. Nothing you measure is transmitted to Field Instruments for storage or analytics.

The design goal is explicit: the tools should work like a scientific instrument that lives inside your browser, with the privacy boundary drawn at your device.

AdSense and Google DoubleClick DART cookies

Field Instruments displays content through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, including the DoubleClick Data and Remarketing (DART) cookie, to serve and limit advertisements. These cookies are governed by Google's own privacy policy. We do not share personally identifying information with advertisers.

Advertising is optional at the page level: you can block or disable personalized advertising through your Google account settings or through the consent tools provided by the platform.

What we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, precise location data, health data, or the contents of your microphone beyond in-memory acoustic analysis. No accounts are required.

Security and retention

Because no personal data leaves the device, retention risk is minimal. Session data remains in memory until the tab closes.

Educational use: This tool is for educational purposes and should not replace professional calibration equipment.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact address on our Contact page.
