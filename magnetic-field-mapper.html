Scientific principle

Reading the live charts

A magnetometer measures the vector magnetic field with Hall or magnetoresistive sensing, reporting each axis and a total magnitude. Compared with Earth's field of roughly 25 to 65 microteslas, deviations reveal anomalies caused by ferrous objects or subsurface geology.

Practical applications

The technique supports geology and mineral exploration, archaeology, navigation calibration, locating buried ferrous objects and general magnetic surveying.

Accuracy and disclaimer

This tool is for educational purposes and should not replace professional calibration equipment. Calibration, offsets and local magnetic interference affect readings.
