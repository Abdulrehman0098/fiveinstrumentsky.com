Frequently Asked Questions

Privacy, sensors, accuracy, and offline use.

No. Field Instruments operates entirely on your device. All calculations and sensor readings happen locally in your browser. No data is ever sent to a server or tracked.

For full functionality, your device needs a Magnetometer (Compass), Gyroscope, Accelerometer, and a Microphone.

Accuracy depends on your device's hardware. Modern smartphones typically provide ±1–5° accuracy for compass readings, while barometric altitude is usually accurate to within 1–2 meters.

Yes. Once the page is loaded, the instruments run entirely client-side. You can disconnect from the internet and continue using the tools.

If the compass reading seems off, move your device in a figure-8 motion a few times. This helps the magnetometer recalibrate to its environment.
